Frequently Asked Questions

What is PrepKit?

PrepKit helps you organize household supplies, including pantry items, refrigerated and frozen food, and emergency preparedness equipment.

Do I need an account?

No. PrepKit is designed to work without registration or a user account.

Does PrepKit work offline?

Yes. You can manage locally saved inventory items without an internet connection. Online product lookup requires internet access.

How does barcode scanning work?

Open the scanner, point your camera at a product barcode, and PrepKit will attempt to identify the product using an online product database. You can also add products manually.

Can I create my own categories?

Yes. PrepKit supports custom categories to help you organize your supplies.

How do expiration reminders work?

You can choose to receive notifications before products expire. Reminder options can be configured in the app's settings.

What happens if I change phones?

Inventory data is stored locally. If your version of PrepKit supports backup export, save a backup before changing devices and import it on your new phone.

How can I report a bug?

Email us with a description of the issue, your device model and app version. Screenshots are helpful but optional.
