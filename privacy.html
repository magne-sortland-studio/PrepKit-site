Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Your inventory belongs to you.

PrepKit is designed to keep your inventory information on your device, without requiring an account or cloud synchronization.

1. Introduction

PrepKit is a mobile application for managing household inventory and emergency preparedness supplies.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use PrepKit and its related website.

For privacy questions, contact the PrepKit developer at magne.sortland.studio@gmail.com .

2. Inventory data

PrepKit stores information you enter, such as product names, quantities, categories, barcodes, expiration dates and notes, locally on your device.

The app does not require an account. Inventory information is not intended to be uploaded to developer-operated servers.

Device backups may include application data, depending on your operating system and backup settings.

3. Camera and barcode scanning

PrepKit may request camera permission when you choose to scan a product barcode.

The camera is used to recognize barcodes. PrepKit does not need to upload camera images to its own servers for this feature.

Camera permission is optional. You can continue adding items manually without using the camera.

4. Third-party product lookup

When you scan a barcode and request product identification, PrepKit may send the barcode to Open Food Facts to retrieve product information.

Open Food Facts may process technical information associated with the request, such as your IP address, according to its own privacy practices.

Learn more at Open Food Facts .

5. Notifications

PrepKit may request permission to send local notifications reminding you when items are approaching their expiration dates.

These reminders are intended to be scheduled on your device without requiring a remote notification server.

You can change notification preferences in the app or disable notifications through your device settings.

6. Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information included in your message. We use this information to respond to your request and handle related support correspondence.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for support, recordkeeping or applicable legal obligations.

7. Advertising and analytics

PrepKit is designed without advertising or third-party behavioral analytics.

If these practices change, this policy will be updated to explain the additional processing.

8. Data storage and deletion

Inventory information is stored locally until you edit or delete it, clear application data, or uninstall the app.

Exported backups and operating-system backups may remain in locations you control until you delete them separately.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable privacy law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information processed by the developer.

For information stored only on your device, you can manage that information through the app. For support correspondence or other information held by us, contact the email address below.

10. Children's privacy

PrepKit is designed as a general household organization tool and is not specifically directed toward children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes or legal requirements change. The latest version will be available on this page.
